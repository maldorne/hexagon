Advice for whoever just arrived

The villages all lie to the south. To reach Naduk, the nearest one, follow the road south to the crossing and go east from there. To the north there are only the temple and the adventurer's house. The forest, beyond the villages, is no place for somebody who has just started.

To move and to look around:

  look                  the place you are in
  look <something>      an object, a creature or a person
  north, south, east... you move that way
  map                   what you know of the area

To know how you are doing:

  sheet                 every detail of your character
  score                 a few plain figures, in short
  inventory             what you are carrying
  condition             how your health is holding up

To talk and to fight:

  say <anything>        you say it out loud
  consider <creature>   how a fight with it would go
  attack <creature>     you start the fight

And when you get lost:

  commands              everything you can do
  help <command>        what one of them does

Two warnings that save grief. First: look at a creature and consider it before attacking, because many of the ones in the forest will kill a newcomer without effort. Second: if you run out of health you die, and coming back costs. Flee before it gets that far.
